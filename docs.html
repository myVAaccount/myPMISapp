📘 Part I: Early Warning & Risk System Guide

The Early Warning Engine automatically scans live shipments, procurement lead times, fuel stock runways, worker safety licenses, and camp housing every time data syncs.

Warning Name Module in App Severity When Does It Trigger? Impact on Project
1. Delayed Shipment 🚚 Transport & Cost ⛔ Critical Cargo unarrived past Expected Date + Grace Period (2 days). Construction work depending on materials is halted.
2. Route Weather Alert 🚚 Transport & Cost ⛔ Critical / ⚠ Warning Corridor has Delayed (typhoon/port shut) or Watch (gale warning). Barges & trucks stranded; crew shift changes delayed.
3. Must-Order-By Passed 📦 Procurement ⛔ Critical Calendar date is past Must-Order-By Date and status is not Ordered. Materials arrive after task start date; site stoppage.
4. Expiring Certification 👥 Workforce ⛔ Critical / ⚠ Warning Safety/equipment license expires within 30 days (Warn) or 7 days (Crit). Worker is legally disqualified by DOLE/safety rules.
5. Low Fuel Stock ⛽ Transport & Cost ⛔ Critical / ⚠ Warning Diesel storage down to 7 days (Warn) or 3 days (Crit). Site generators and heavy excavators run out of power.
6. Budget Cost Overrun 💰 Cost & Schedule ⚠ Warning Committed + In Transit + Installed exceeds approved Budget. Activity is losing money; erodes project contingency.
7. Camp Bed Road Delay 👥 Workforce / Camp Beds ⛔ Critical / ⚠ Warning Outbound road delay activates +24h stay extension, or same-day shift handover. Prevents double-booking (hot-bedding) and keeps stranded crew housed safely.

Rule 1: Delayed Shipment Behind Checkpoint

⛔ Critical
📱 Fields in App Used:
  • Cargo / Material: Equipment or materials being shipped (e.g. Cone Crusher Liners).
  • Expected Date: Target arrival date agreed with freight carrier.
  • Status: Tracking stage (Not Started, In Transit, At Checkpoint, Delivered).
  • Alert Grace Period: Configurable delay buffer under Settings (default: 2 days).
  • Linked Activity: Construction or maintenance activity waiting for this shipment.
⏱️ When Does It Occur?

Turns RED (Critical) when cargo is still marked "In Transit" or "At Checkpoint" and the calendar has passed the Expected Date by more than 2 days.

Plain Example: Expected arrival is August 20. Grace period is 2 days (Aug 22). On August 23, if status remains In Transit, the delayed alert immediately triggers and flags the linked activity (e.g. "Affects activity: Primary Crusher Mechanical Erection").
✅ Action Required:
  1. Contact shipping agent or barge captain for GPS checkpoint coordinates.
  2. Reschedule downstream labor crews to avoid paid idle standby time.

Rule 2: Route Weather Alert

⛔ Critical ⚠ Warning
📱 Fields in App Used:
  • Weather: Maritime or highway corridor state (None, Watch, Delayed).
  • Route: Travel corridor (e.g. Cebu Port → Lipata Port → Site Laydown).
  • Current Leg Mode: Active transport mode (Truck, Barge/Boat, Final-Mile Road).
⏱️ When Does It Occur?
  • ⛔ Critical (Red): Route is marked "Delayed" (Coast Guard suspended sailings due to Typhoon Signal, or landslide road blockage).
  • ⚠ Warning (Amber): Route is on "Watch" (Gale warning, rough sea advisory within 24–48 hours).
💥 What Happens on Site: Sea barges must anchor, incurring daily demurrage fees. Stranded workers awaiting crew rotation get stuck at transit ports.
✅ Action Required: Secure sensitive cargo at regional transit hubs and arrange temporary hotel lodging for stranded personnel at transit ports.

Rule 3: Must-Order-By Date Passed (Procurement Lead Time)

⛔ Critical
📱 Fields in App Used:
  • Item: Material or equipment description.
  • Need By: Required date when material must be physically on site ready for work.
  • Supplier Lead (Days): Factory manufacturing & fabrication duration.
  • Transport Lead (Days): Inter-island shipping & haulage duration.
  • Buffer (Days): Contingency safety days for customs/weather holds.
📐 Formula: Must Order By Date = Need By Date − (Supplier Lead + Transport Lead + Buffer Days)
⏱️ When Does It Occur?

Triggers RED (Critical) when today's date has passed the calculated Must-Order-By Date and status has not yet been marked as "Ordered".

Plain Example: Need steel plates on Dec 1. Supplier Lead = 30d, Shipping = 15d, Buffer = 5d (Total Lead = 50d). The Must-Order-By Date is October 12. On Oct 13, if unreleased, an emergency procurement alert is raised.
✅ Action Required: Release PO to Primary Supplier immediately, or switch to the pre-qualified Backup Supplier in the Supplier Directory.

Rule 4: Expiring Worker Certification

⛔ Critical ⚠ Warning
📱 Fields in App Used:
  • Worker & Trade: Full name and technical specialty (e.g. Tower Crane Operator).
  • Certification: Mandatory government or safety license (e.g. TESDA Crane NC II).
  • Cert Expiry: License expiration date.
  • Rotation Out: Scheduled fly-out date for rest rotation.
  • Alert Window: Notification lead time configured in Settings (standard: 30 days).
⏱️ When Does It Occur?
  • ⛔ Critical (Red): 7 days or fewer remaining before certificate expiry.
  • ⚠ Warning (Amber): Between 8 and 30 days remaining before expiry.
💥 What Happens on Site: Philippine DOLE and Mine Safety rules prohibit uncertified operators from operating heavy plant machinery. An expired license halts crane or earthmoving operations immediately.
✅ Action Required: Check if worker departs on rotation before expiry date. Schedule renewal test during off-rotation or mobilize a certified backup technician.

Rule 5: Low Fuel Stock (Site Autonomy Depletion)

⛔ Critical ⚠ Warning
📱 Fields in App Used:
  • Current Fuel Stock (L): Total diesel volume currently inside main storage tanks.
  • Liters Consumed: Daily burn logs from power generators and heavy fleet.
  • Average Daily Consumption: Calculated historical daily burn rate.
📐 Formula: Days of Fuel Remaining = Current Tank Stock (L) ÷ Average Daily Consumption (L/day)
⏱️ When Does It Occur?
  • ⛔ Critical (Red): Remaining fuel runway is 3 days or less.
  • ⚠ Warning (Amber): Remaining fuel runway is between 4 and 7 days.
Plain Example: Storage has 28,000 Liters. Average burn is 7,000 Liters/day. Remaining runway = 4 days. System flags an immediate Fuel Warning.
✅ Action Required: Expedite fuel tanker barge / road tanker delivery immediately. If delayed, shed non-critical camp loads and conserve diesel for primary processing power.

Rule 6: Activity Budget Cost Overrun

⚠ Warning
📱 Fields in App Used:
  • Activity & Cost Code: Task name and ledger code (e.g. Primary Crusher Foundation / C-101).
  • Budget: Approved financial cap.
  • Committed: Signed contracts and POs issued.
  • In Transit: Freight & logistics expenditure en route.
  • Installed / Earned: Completed, inspected on-site work.
📐 Formula: Total Spend = Committed + In Transit + Installed  |  Variance = Budget − Total Spend
⏱️ When Does It Occur?

Triggers Amber (Warning) whenever Total Spend exceeds Budget (resulting in a negative variance shown in red in the app).

Plain Example: Budget is ₱1,000,000. Committed = ₱500k, In-Transit = ₱200k, Installed = ₱400k (Total = ₱1,100,000). Activity is ₱100,000 over budget.
✅ Action Required: Audit contractor billings for unauthorized scope creep and submit a formal change order request to project management.

Rule 7: Camp Bed Road Delay & Handover (Anti-Hot-Bedding)

⛔ Critical ⚠ Warning
📱 Fields in App Used:
  • Room / Bed ID: Physical bunk identifier (e.g. MH-A-01).
  • Current Occupant: Worker currently sleeping in the bed.
  • Vacating Date: Planned fly-out date.
  • Road Delay Safety Extension (+24h Ext): Emergency toggle activated when road/weather stops outbound convoys.
  • Bed Status: 🔴 Occupied, 🟡 Pending Handover, 🔵 Housekeeping, 🟢 Available.
⏱️ When Does It Occur?
  • ⛔ Critical (Red) — Road Delay Bed Extension: Active when road mud/weather halts transport, automatically extending outbound occupant stay by +24 hours to prevent unhousing personnel.
  • ⚠ Warning (Amber) — Same-Day Bed Turnover: Active when bed is Pending Handover and vacating date is Today.
💥 What Happens on Site: Eliminates dangerous "hot-bedding" (double-booking uncleaned beds) and prevents leaving outbound workers stranded in weather without accommodation.
✅ Action Required: Execute 3-step turnover protocol:
  1. When worker leaves, click "Send to Housekeeping" (🔵).
  2. Housekeeping inspects and disinfects linen, then clicks "Sanitation Cleared" (🟢).
  3. Front desk clicks "Check-In Inbound" (🔴) only after green clearance.

📅 Bonus Reference: Daily Attendance & Time Tracking

Workforce Module
📱 Key Attendance Fields:
  • Selected Date: Filter logs by Today, Yesterday, or any date on the calendar.
  • Attendance Status: 🟢 Present (Full day), 🟡 Late (Partial day), 🔴 Absent, ⚪ Off Rotation.
  • Time In & Out: Shift clock times (e.g. 07:00 to 17:00).
  • Calculated Shift Hours: Automated formula: Time Out − Time In (e.g. 10.0 hrs).
  • Work Area Notes: Specific site location (e.g. Crusher foundation formwork, Night OT).
